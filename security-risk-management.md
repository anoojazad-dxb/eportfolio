Welcome to my module repository for **Security Risk Management** at the University of Essex. This page documents my academic work, team project contributions, collaborative discussion summaries, and reflective practice across the 12 units of this module.

## 1. Summary of Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of this module, I am able to:

1. **Analyse Risks & Vulnerabilities:** Identify and analyse critically security risks, threats, and vulnerabilities in information systems, accounting for the current threat landscape.
2. **Synthesise Security Intelligence:** Gather and synthesise information from multiple sources (including internet security alerts and warning sites) to aid in the systematic analysis of risks and security issues.
3. **Determine Mitigation Methodologies:** Critically determine appropriate methodologies, tools, and techniques (e.g., NIST SP 800-30, STRIDE) to mitigate and solve security risks and their business impact.
4. **Evaluate Professional & Ethical Issues:** Articulate the legal, social, ethical, and professional issues faced by information security and risk professionals.
